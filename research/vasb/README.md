# Verifiable Agent Safety Benchmark research record

VASB was a standalone research project about how evaluators distinguish proposed actions from committed effects and decide what incomplete observations support. This SVB component records its final results, limitations, and provenance. The complete working archive and its original Git history are held privately. This component consists of a newly written factual summary, source links, and cryptographic identifiers. [Provenance and verification record](PROVENANCE.json)

## What the archive established

The original project included a scripted prototype, sandboxed local-model experiments, observer comparisons, pinned AgentDojo and ControlArena cases, controlled scorer mutations, and conditional Lean checks. The final recorded replay passed 233 distinct historical tests and matched 115 historical SQLite outcomes. A separate checker matched 69 references and findings, detected the 13 selected mutations, and made zero defect allegations on 16 selected controls. Eighteen correction tests passed, and 14 native AgentDojo regression records matched their archived results. Lean 4.28.0 recompiled the historical lemmas, propositions, and a certificate for a finite set of exported comparisons.

Under the stated fixtures, intermediate state distinguished achieved Slack membership changes from failed tool requests, and a source-aware comparator resolved the canonical Banking Task1 source binding. The upstream reports predate these replications. AgentDojo and ControlArena already supported the stronger observations on the matched comparisons. [Slack reports #168 and #189](https://github.com/ethz-spylab/agentdojo/issues/168) · [Companion Slack report](https://github.com/ethz-spylab/agentdojo/issues/189) · [Banking report #181](https://github.com/ethz-spylab/agentdojo/issues/181) · [Banking report #240](https://github.com/ethz-spylab/agentdojo/issues/240)

A separately versioned witness rule can accept a committed violation when the observed effect and authority facts establish it despite open capture. The rule retains UNKNOWN when the available facts remain insufficient. The original conservative policy and its outcomes are preserved in the private archive. These are conditional results on specified observations and author-selected cases. General reliability, real-world capture completeness, independent human validation, novel methodology, and methodological superiority remain unestablished.

Contemporaneous work covers executable contracts and evidence-supported scores. VASB's contribution is the recorded replication and integration of established methods. The references are links to the original sources.
