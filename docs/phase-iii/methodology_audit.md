# Internal critical audit of Phase IIb

This audit was conducted after seeing the historical results. It is internal
author analysis, not an independent external review. Separate implementations
can challenge arithmetic and scoring; they do not create human scientific
adjudication. All old protocols, scores and outputs remain untouched.

## Findings that most constrain interpretation

**1. Six-way exact agreement is not a pure scientific-reasoning construct.**
The categories mix methods and verdicts, and the standard/reversed development
gate also mixes decision stability with justification-type stability. A new
post-hoc replay finds:

| Model | Same exact category after reversal / 6 | Same coarse decision / 6 | Changes within accepting group |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5B | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| 1.5B | 3 | 6 | 3 |
| 3B | 2 | 3 | 1 |

Thus 1.5B's three label changes preserve acceptance/nonacceptance. That does not
make the evidence types correct: calling a deductive axiom result empirical can
still be inappropriate. It does show that the failed order gate alone does not
establish unstable scientific acceptance. No threshold is relaxed and no candidate
is retrospectively requalified. Future qualification should separately test
format, evidence-type attribution and target-verdict stability on expert-qualified
development material. The tiny toy/historical controls are not a representative
sample of scientific comprehension.

**2. False acceptance is operationally meaningful but conditional.**
The fixed contract asks about the exact target, so an accepting category on an
unsupported/refuted target is an observable inappropriate acceptance under that
contract. Multiplicity among formal/computational/empirical positive categories
cannot change the indicator. The executed version-0.4 post-hoc invariance audit
confirms all published evidence-present counts in both decoding modes: 0.5B
1/6, 1.5B 2/6. This does not validate the references externally.

A disagreement between refuted and insufficient likewise leaves acceptance
membership unchanged, though it changes exact scientific interpretation. If
actual reviewers disagree across the acceptance boundary, a single unqualified
false-accept count is no longer identified. Report definite versus ambiguous
items and fixed-denominator indicator bounds under a new protocol. Do not choose
an alternate reference after seeing which model benefits. No revised gold or
model score is produced here.

Lower false acceptance does not imply better usefulness. On the six supported
cases, 0.5B accepts only one and 1.5B three. Abstention and incorrect rejection
contribute to the tradeoff. Neither the unsupported numerical baseline's zero
coverage nor rich C's zero admission establishes safety. “False acceptance” here
means a categorical decision, not an inferred calibrated probability or a
comprehensive scientific harm rate.

**3. Atomic targets can be interpretation-sensitive.**
“Data establish X” contains a meta-inferential claim. A reviewer can reject the
inference while leaving X's truth unknown. Treating that as refuted versus
insufficient is a substantive rubric choice. Strong claims using exactly,
uniquely, proves or every future patient also offer superficial lexical cues.
No final-set neutral-paraphrase or row-permutation study was executed. Paired
claims improve diagnosis but do not by themselves isolate scientific reasoning
from recognition of skeptical wording.

**4. Dependence and exposure limit generalization.**
Twelve items share only three studies, paired items share documents and category
order, and all new studies share R curation. They are not twelve independent
experiments or diverse source-provider samples. Four conditions/modes per item
are repeated observations, not independent replication. Development and final
study families differ, but public source pretraining and author access remain
unknown. Hashing and prompt filtering provide integrity/isolation, not hidden
custody, prospective independence or external preregistration.

No iid p-values or confidence intervals are appropriate for a broad scientific-AI
population claim here. The retained historical C scorer includes a descriptive
Wilson field; retaining it does not endorse its use on clustered items. Runtime
numbers are one shared-CPU measurement, not a hardware-controlled performance
comparison. Two final models from one family do not identify a causal size effect.

**5. Source completeness and reference correctness are different checks.**
A separate integer-tenths/cross-product implementation reading archived CSVs
reproduces the sleep mean (79/50 hours), nine positive pairs/one tie, chick
sample comparison (2189/36 g), 42→205 g growth and oxygen conversion (23/2000 g/L).
It does not import the prior numerical oracle/parser. No new numerical discrepancy
was found in those checks. Source hashes and source transformations were also
validated by the existing independent core.

That is not a finding that every reference answer is correct. Original sleep
ordering/washout, chick assignment/follow-up, and BOD sampling/measurement methods
were not independently reconstructed. The unit contradiction is precise for
stored inputs; source physical accuracy is separate. Rounded FreeSolv equality
can be a recorded fact and computational comparison; its experimental 0.60 field
is assigned by default, not an independently known standard error. The proposed
pilot requires experts to challenge scientific scope and evidence-type labels,
including whether primary-method gaps make an item unassessable.

**6. The schema confound was reduced, not fully causally isolated.**
A admits all evidence-present categories, making actual categorical errors
observable. C remains all malformed; its historical scoring status normalizes
them to execution_error despite completed generation. A/C differs in ordering,
length and instructions beyond output schema. Constrained A changes legal token
paths and can alter category selection. Neither comparison estimates a pure
causal effect of schema burden or proves improved reasoning.

**7. Prose and confidence claims require external judgment.**
B requests attribution but both models omit bracketed IDs. Zero nonsupplied IDs
is therefore vacuous, not successful source use. Correct limitations coexist with
false assertions or contradictions, and one response is capped. Human reasoning
accuracy remains null. A contains no probabilities; C has no valid distributions,
so calibration and unsupported-certainty rates cannot be inferred. Nonempty prose
is not a reasoning score or evidence of internal faithfulness.

## Consequences

The supported finding is a reproducible separation of observable interface,
category, arithmetic and scope failures on specific public packets. H1–H4,
benchmark superiority, representative scientific competence and expert validity
remain unconfirmed. The taxonomy pilot may reject or materially revise the task.
That outcome should lead to a new rubric/protocol, not retroactive favorable
rescoring. The next scientific observation should be actual independent review,
not another larger internally labeled dataset or unqualified model sweep.
